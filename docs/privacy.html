Privacy Policy

Shadow Gasp — true-crime and mystery comics · Last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy explains what information Shadow Gasp ("we", "us") receives when you interact with our Facebook Page or Instagram account, and how we use it. It covers our messaging app, which is connected to Meta's platforms (Facebook and Instagram), and our Pinterest app, which publishes our own artwork to our own Pinterest account. It does not cover Meta's own processing, which is described in Meta's Privacy Policy, or purchases on Gumroad, which are covered by Gumroad's Privacy Policy.

What our app does

When you comment the word "COMIC" on a post by our Facebook Page or Instagram account, our app may send you one private message offering a free digital copy of issue #1 of our comic in exchange for an honest review. If you reply and choose "Yes", we send you a one-time link to that free copy. If you choose "Not right now", we send a short thank-you. We do not message anyone who has not commented the keyword, and we do not send unsolicited promotional messages.

Our Pinterest app

We also run a small application that uses the Pinterest API to publish Pins to our own Pinterest business account. Each Pin shows artwork we created and own, and links to the matching product page in our Gumroad store. Only the owner of that Pinterest account authorises the application, through Pinterest's standard OAuth consent screen, and the resulting access token is kept in the owner's local environment and in encrypted build secrets. It is never shared with anyone else.

This application does not collect, process or store personal data belonging to other Pinterest users. It reads only our own account name, our own boards and the performance figures for our own Pins, and it writes only Pins to our own boards. If you interact with one of our Pins on Pinterest, that interaction is handled by Pinterest under Pinterest's Privacy Policy, not by us.

How we use it

Only to run the conversation described above. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or selling, and we do not combine it with other data.

Who can access it

We do not sell or share your information. It is processed by two service providers that run our app: Cloudflare (hosting and storage) and GitHub (which runs the job that sends the message). If you choose to claim the free copy, you open a link on Gumroad, our store; anything you enter there is handled under Gumroad's privacy policy.

Emails and our newsletter

Our messages may include a link to follow Shadow Gasp on Gumroad for new releases. Joining is optional, and every email sent through Gumroad includes an unsubscribe link.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can ask us to delete the information above at any time. See our Data Deletion instructions. You can also stop the conversation at any time simply by not replying, and you can block or remove our Page or account in Facebook or Instagram.

Children

Our comics cover true-crime themes and are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Send a message to the Shadow Gasp Page on Facebook or to Shadow Gasp on Instagram, and we will reply.
