Shadow Gasp Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-31

Shadow Gasp Publisher is a personal-use application that connects Shadow Gasp's own Pinterest account to an automated publishing tool, for the sole purpose of posting promotional content for Shadow Gasp's own comics and reviewing that content's performance.

What this app accesses

This app only ever accesses the Pinterest account it is directly authorized on — its own Pins, Boards, and the view/engagement data for that same account. It does not access, request, or store data belonging to any other Pinterest user.

What this app does with that access

  • Creates Pins on Shadow Gasp's own boards to promote Shadow Gasp comics.
  • Reads view/engagement counts on Shadow Gasp's own existing Pins, to review and prune older, low-performing posts.

Data sharing

No data obtained through this integration is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. It is used only internally, by Shadow Gasp, to operate its own Pinterest account.

Data retention

No Pinterest account data is stored outside of Pinterest itself beyond what is needed transiently to perform the actions above.
