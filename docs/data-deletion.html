Data Deletion Instructions

Shadow Gasp — true-crime and mystery comics · Last updated: 15 September 2026

If you commented on a Shadow Gasp post or messaged us, our app may hold a small amount of information about you: your page- or Instagram-scoped user ID, your username or first name, the ID of your comment, and a marker showing whether you received a free copy. The details are in our Privacy Policy. Most of it is deleted automatically within 30 days.

How to ask us to delete your data

  1. Send a message to the Shadow Gasp Page on Facebook, or to Shadow Gasp on Instagram, from the account you used.
  2. Write "Delete my data".
  3. We will delete everything our app holds about that account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Removing the app's access

Our app does not log you in or connect to your personal account, so there is nothing to disconnect. You can also block or remove the Shadow Gasp Page or account in Facebook or Instagram at any time.

Purchases and newsletter

If you claimed a free copy or followed us on Gumroad, that information is held by Gumroad. You can unsubscribe using the link in any Gumroad email, or manage it under Gumroad's privacy policy.
